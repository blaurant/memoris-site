How to suggest Memoris, without making it about memory

That first conversation is where most people say yes or no.

Start with something they want.

“You’re always hunting for that recipe. Want to try something that keeps it for you?”

Lead with their goal, not a problem.

Yes

Try it together, once.

Save one real thing, like a grandchild’s school concert, and ask for it back the next day.

Let them own it.

Their phone, their voice, their account. Resist checking up on it.

No

If they say no, wait.

Many people decline the first time. Try again in a few weeks with a different first use.

Ask their clinician to mention it.

A suggestion from a professional often lands differently.
