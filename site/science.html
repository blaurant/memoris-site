The science behind Memoris

Memoris puts well-studied memory techniques into a simple conversation you can have every day.

Five techniques, made daily

Day 0

Planning the moment.

“When I see Anna, I’ll ask about her trip.” Plans like this, captured on a phone, help people remember to act.

In Memoris: it helps you prepare for calls and visits.

Bringing it back, a little later each time.

Recalling something at growing intervals is one of the most studied memory techniques.

In Memoris: what matters comes back after a day, then 3 days, then a week, and so on.

then 3 days

“When I see Anna…”

Keeping it outside your head.

Using a phone or a notebook to hold information is a core strategy in memory rehabilitation guidelines.

In Memoris: it keeps what you tell it, in your words.

Getting it right the first time.

People relearn facts and routines better when they don’t practice mistakes.

In Memoris: it never stores or repeats a wrong guess.

Working on your own goals.

Progress is strongest on the everyday goals people choose for themselves.

In Memoris: the first conversation asks what you want help with.

Why your own life, not puzzles

Research shows that memory practice helps most with what you actually practice; general brain games carry over poorly. That’s why Memoris practices with your own names, places and plans.

Honest about what’s next

These techniques have been studied in clinical research. Memoris is new, and we plan to study it with clinical partners in four steps:

  1. whether people keep using it;

  2. whether they recall their own information better;

  3. whether it helps with their everyday goals;

  4. and a controlled trial.
