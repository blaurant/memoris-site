This Consumer Health Data Privacy Disclosure (“CHD Disclosure”) supplements the Memoris Privacy Policy. This CHD Disclosure is required by the My Health My Data Act (“MHMD”) in Washington and other state consumer health data privacy laws and applies only to personal information that may be considered “Consumer Health Data” under those laws.

1. Categories of Consumer Health Data collected.

As part of providing the Service, Memoris may collect personal information that may be considered Consumer Health Data, such as:

  • medical information (for example, allergies, medications, medical conditions) you choose to provide when interacting with the Service; and
  • relationship data, such as familial or other relationships with third parties whose personal information you may provide to us when interacting with the Service.

2. Sources of Consumer Health Data collected.

We receive this Consumer Health Data from the following sources:

  • directly from you;
  • third-party services you link to the Service; and
  • third parties that collect or provide Consumer Health Data in connection with work they do on our behalf

3. How Consumer Health Data may be used.

We use the Consumer Health Data we collect for the purpose of providing the Service you request from Memoris, including to enable you to record and track important information.

4. How Consumer Health Data may be disclosed.

Memoris does not sell our users’ personal information, including Consumer Health Data, to others. We only disclose Consumer Health Data as directed by you, for the purpose of providing the Service you request from Memoris, or as explained in this section.

As described in our Privacy Policy, Memoris may disclose personal information in connection with a reorganization (for example, as part of a merger with another organization) or for the protection of Memoris or others.

We may share your Consumer Health Data with our corporate affiliates and subsidiaries (including Memoris AI France SASU).

We also employ other companies and individuals to perform functions on our behalf, such as acting on an emergency you report, sending postal mail and email, analyzing data, and providing customer service. These service providers may have access to Consumer Health Data needed to perform their functions but are not permitted to use it for other purposes.

5. How to exercise your rights related to Consumer Health Data.

Memoris offers certain choices to users that you can review in our Privacy Policy. If you are covered by MHMD or other applicable state consumer health data privacy law, then you may have certain additional rights under the law that applies to you, such as the right to access, correct or delete personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@memoris.ai.

You may also have the right to appeal the denial of any of these rights by contacting us at support@memoris.ai. Depending on the rights or choices you exercise, certain services may be limited or unavailable. For security purposes, we will generally ask you to verify your request using the contact information you have already provided. If you do not have an account, please contact us at support@memoris.ai.

6. Updates to this Consumer Health Data Disclosure.

As our business changes, we may also update this CHD Disclosure. If we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this Disclosure, and if we make material changes we will provide you with additional notice as required by law.
