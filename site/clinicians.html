How it works

Support

Patients capture what matters by voice, text or photo, with text recognition and image analysis. Memoris connects it to their calendar and tasks, reminds them at the right time and retrieves it in context when they ask.

Stimulate

At capture, Memoris prompts association, elaboration and intention to deepen encoding. Spaced reactivation then consolidates each memory, and cued recall gives hints before revealing the answer.

How Support and Stimulate work together

One architecture serves both: the same structured memory store powers dependable recall and targeted stimulation. Every Support interaction is also a practice opportunity: what the patient stores becomes the material for encoding and spaced reactivation, so practice is relevant and adherence is higher.

Particularly suited to early-stage neurodegenerative conditions

Memoris is particularly well suited to early-onset dementia and early degenerative diagnoses such as MCI due to Alzheimer’s disease. Patients learn the tool while they are still autonomous, Stimulate supports the abilities they retain, and Support and Connect take over progressively as needs grow: one tool follows the patient through the course of the condition.

What patients gain

Practical

Appointments, tasks and important information stay organized and resurface when they matter; names, stories and plans stay within reach, keeping patients socially engaged.

Psychological

Infinite patience: the tenth question is welcomed as warmly as the first. A dependable backup keeps patients autonomous and secure, easing anxiety and confusion, and every successful recall is a small victory that rebuilds confidence.

The virtuous cycle

Practical wins build confidence, and confidence keeps patients engaging: the cycle memory care depends on.
