Support

It finds everything for you

  1. You tell it.

    By speaking, writing, or with a photo. A name, an appointment, a story: one sentence is enough.

  2. It remembers.

    Memoris keeps and organizes everything for you. Nothing to file, nothing to memorize.

    Yours, kept

  3. It answers you.

    You ask, and it gives it back right away, in a few simple words.

    “Where did I put my keys?” In the pocket of your gray coat. You put them there this morning.

Soon, it will also come to you on its own: a gentle reminder at the right moment, without you even having to ask.

Stimulate

It helps you hold on to what matters

Memoris does not just remember in your place. It helps you practice remembering what matters. When you ask, it can give you a hint first so you find it yourself, with the full answer always one step away.

  2. You

    “What is my grandson Paul’s girlfriend’s name?”

  3. Memoris

    Let’s find it together. Who gave you this information? Where were you?

  4. You

    “It was Paul, he came for lunch with her at my place last weekend to introduce her to me.”

  5. Memoris

    Can you try to recall that moment? Take a moment to think about it, then describe it to me.

  6. You

    “I can picture us chatting at the table.”

  7. Memoris

    What were you feeling at that moment?

  8. You

    “I feel moved because they look so much in love. Paul speaks to her so kindly. I can see him offering her a little wine.”

  9. You

    “Oh yes, that is it, he says to her: ‘Alex, may I pour you some more?’ There it is, I remember, her name is Alex.”
